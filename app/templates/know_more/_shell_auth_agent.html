Navigation, Sign-In, Agent Brain & Multi-Device Access

A visual, everyday guide to every button, search shortcut, sign-in option, and safety feature across the JKLU CareerOS workspace—from switching themes and jumping to students to inspecting the live AI Agent Brain.

Everyone Visual UI Guide
Everyday Flow: 1. Sign In with @jklu.edu.in → 2. Pick Student or Role View → 3. Jump Anywhere with Ctrl+K → 4. Inspect Live AI Steps in Agent Brain
1.1

Left Sidebar, Topbar, Quick Search & Dark Mode

Everyone
Jump to a student, page or action… Ctrl K
Everyone

Where to find it: Top Bar → Left / Center search bar (or press Ctrl+K / Cmd+K anywhere)

What happens when you use it: Type a keyword and press Enter to jump straight to any screen or run an admin cleanup:

  • copilot → Opens Placement Copilot (Everyone)
  • home → Opens the Home cohort directory (Everyone)
  • approval → Opens Approvals queue (Faculty & TPO Admin)
  • admin or analytic → Opens Cohort Analytics (Faculty & TPO Admin)
  • audit → Opens the Audit Trail ledger (TPO Admin only)
  • know more, knowmore, guide, manual, or docs → Opens this Guide (TPO Admin only)
  • purge, clear live, or remove live → Removes all scraped live market jobs after a confirmation pop-up while keeping on-campus jobs safe (TPO Admin only)
  • Any student’s name → Jumps directly to that student’s profile page

JKLU Institutional Sign-In & Access Modes

Everyone
Everyone

Where to find it: Sign-In Page → Main form below Institutional email & PIN

What happens when you click it: Checks that your email ends with @jklu.edu.in and verifies your institutional PIN securely offline (no external internet login required). Once verified, it opens your role’s home workspace and records the sign-in in the audit ledger.

Demo Mode Only

Where to find it: Sign-In Page → 1-Click Judge Demo Personas, Student personas list, and Institutional role fast login

What happens when you click it: Signs you in immediately as a pre-configured Student, Faculty Mentor, or TPO Admin persona for quick evaluation—no manual typing needed. These cards are automatically hidden whenever Strict institutional lock is turned on.

Demo Mode vs. Strict Institutional Mode

Demo Mode (Evaluation & Training)

Shows the Top Bar role switcher and 1-Click Fast Login persona cards so reviewers can test Student, Faculty, and TPO Admin views effortlessly.

Strict Mode (Production Lock)

Requires valid @jklu.edu.in email + PIN sign-in for every page, hides all Fast Login and role-switcher shortcuts, locks students out of other students’ profiles, and enforces automatic session sign-out on logout.

What happens when you click it: Opens the tamper-evident Audit Trail filtered directly to the selected AI run so administrators can inspect every recorded action and verification check.

Built-In Safety & Privacy Checks (Always On)

  • No Fake Numbers or Claims: Every score, policy rule, and job reference is cross-checked against real database records and official placement rules, showing verified badges in Agent Brain.
  • Personal Info Protection: Phone numbers, emails, Aadhaar/PAN numbers, and street addresses are automatically masked before resumes are stored or sent to any AI model.
  • Tamper & Injection Defense: Hidden instructions inside uploaded resumes, scraped job posts, or interview answers are automatically blocked and logged before reaching the AI.
1.4

Offline PWA, Desktop App & Mobile App

Everyone
Installable Browser App (PWA)

Install JKLU CareerOS directly from your browser’s address bar. Visual assets, fonts, and charts load instantly from local cache, while sensitive sign-in and role pages are never cached so switching accounts or signing out stays completely private. If your connection drops, a clean offline notice appears.

Windows Desktop App

Runs JKLU CareerOS as a standalone Windows desktop application (available as a portable app or installer) with a branded startup splash screen and full access to the same placement portal.

Mobile App (Android)

Packages the responsive JKLU CareerOS experience as a native mobile app so students and staff can check job matches, upskill plans, and approvals on the go.
