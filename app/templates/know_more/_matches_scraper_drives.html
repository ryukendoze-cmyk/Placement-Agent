How Every Job's Match Score Is Calculated
1. Composite Fit Score (0–100) 60 / 20 / 20
  • 60% Required Skills & Depth: Counts how many required skills the student has, checking if their depth matches the role (standard roles expect Intermediate; senior roles expect Advanced). Being one level below earns partial credit.
  • 20% Preferred Skills Bonus: Rewards matching nice-to-have skills listed by the employer.
  • 20% Overall Profile Relevance: Measures how closely the student's overall resume story aligns with the full job description.
2. Trust Bonus & Experience Guard Fairness Rules
  • Verified Skill Trust: Skills proven in a quiz or mock interview earn 100% credit; resume-only skills earn 85%; skills needing re-verification earn 50%; unverified skills earn 20%.
  • Proficiency Bonus (up to +10 pts): Extra points when matched required skills are verified at Intermediate or Advanced levels.
  • Seniority Deduction (-25 pts): Automatically deducts 25 points if an undergrad/fresher (< 2 yrs experience) is scored against a Senior, Lead, Staff, or 3+ year role.
3. Eligibility & Salary Tier Policy Checks
  • 4 Eligibility Checks: Verifies (a) role isn't senior-only for freshers, (b) student GPA meets the cutoff, (c) student branch is allowed (blank = open to all), and (d) active backlogs don't exceed policy limits.
  • Score Bands: Strong (80%+), Moderate / Good (60–79%), Developing (40–59%), Early (<40%).
  • Salary Tiers (Disclosed Full-Time): Super Dream (≥ 15 LPA), Dream (8–14.99 LPA), Standard (< 8 LPA).

The 4 Application & Waiver Approval Paths
Path 1 · Instant

Eligible + Standard Tier

Automatically approved and marked Nominated immediately — no manual review wait needed.

Path 2 · Tier 2 Review

Eligible + Dream / Super Dream

Creates a Tier 2: Awaiting TPO Approval request so the TPO Admin can verify high-tier placement rules before nominating.

Path 3 · Blocked

Ineligible (No Waiver)

Blocked by placement policy and lists the exact barriers (GPA cutoff, branch restriction, active backlogs, or senior experience requirement).

Path 4 · 2-Tier Waiver

Ineligible + Request Waiver

Enters the 2-Tier human review queue: requires Tier 1: Faculty Mentor Endorsement first, followed by Tier 2: TPO Final Sign-Off.

Automatic Quality & Data Cleanup Behind the Scenes

Live Link Verification: Company career boards (such as Ashby) are automatically checked before importing so disabled or broken “404 Page Not Found” links are skipped.
USD-to-LPA Salary Conversion: Overseas USD salaries are converted into Indian Lakhs Per Annum (LPA), monthly pay is annualized, and jobs without stated pay stay clearly marked as undisclosed instead of guessing.
Duplicate & Skill Cleanup: Tracking links and duplicate postings across boards are merged, placeholder links are blocked, and noisy skill tags are verified against the actual job description and 124-skill catalog. Student-fetched jobs stay private to that student's workspace.
