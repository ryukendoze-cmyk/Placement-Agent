Job Matching Engine & Incremental Auto-Rank

Hybrid semantic + skill-coverage scoring, verification & depth weighting, SOP eligibility gates, and incremental page-load ranking

app/matching/engine.py · rank.py · pages.py · students.py
Composite Score Formula (score_job)

base = round(100 * (0.60 * coverage + 0.20 * pref_score + 0.20 * semantic_sim)) from WEIGHTS = {"coverage": 0.6, "preferred": 0.2, "semantic": 0.2}. Final score is max(0, min(100, base + proficiency_bonus + seniority_penalty)).

  • Depth Weight (depth_weight): target_depth_for(job) is "advanced" when is_senior_job(job) is true, else "intermediate". Exact/higher depth = 1.0; 1 level below = 0.4; 2 levels below = 0.1.
  • Verification Multipliers (VERIFICATION_WEIGHTS): verified = 1.0, claimed = 0.85, needs_reverification = 0.5, unverified = 0.2 (effective depth of unverified skills is also capped at "basic").
  • Proficiency Bonus (_proficiency_bonus): PROFICIENCY_POINTS (basic: 0, intermediate: 5, advanced: 10) multiplied by verification weight, averaged across required skills, capped at min(10, round(pts / req_count)).
  • Seniority Penalty (SENIORITY_PENALTY = -25): Applied when is_campus_fresher(student) (year ≤ 4 or < 2 yrs experience) is scored against is_senior_job(job) (Senior/Lead/Staff/III+ title or experience_years >= 3.0).
Bands, Eligibility, Tiers & Visibility
  • Bands (band_for): Reads SOP-05 min_coverage_pct (default 40): strong (≥ max(80, floor)), moderate (≥ 60), developing (≥ 40), early (< 40).
  • Eligibility Gate (job_eligibility): Evaluates (1) campus fresher vs senior role lock (SENIOR_TITLE_RE / exp >= 3), (2) student.gpa < job.min_gpa, (3) allowed_branches via is_branch_eligible (empty list = open to all branches), (4) student.active_backlogs > max_backlogs per SOP-03. Returns (bool, reasons[]).
  • Policy Tier (job_tier): Uses SOP-01 rules via top-end LPA — super_dream (≥ 15.0 LPA), dream (≥ 8.0 LPA), standard (< 8.0 LPA or undisclosed fallback).
  • Visibility Scope (is_job_visible_to_student): On-campus jobs (is_on_campus_job: source == "local", scraper_engine == "campus_drive", or non-job-ext-* IDs) and unowned scraped jobs (owner_student_id is None) are visible to all; student-scraped jobs (owner_student_id == sid) stay scoped to their creator.
