How the Planner Picks the Smartest Courses

Automatic Schedule Optimization
1. Estimates Current Skill Mastery

Verified skills start with high confidence, while unverified or flagged resume claims start lower. Skills with the biggest gap are prioritized first.

2. Respects Prerequisites & Weekly Cap

Foundational topics are scheduled before advanced ones, and long courses are split cleanly across weeks so you never exceed your weekly hour limit.

3. Highlights Biggest Score Gains per Hour

Every course shows its expected match-score boost per study hour and how many campus drives it helps unlock, so high-impact skills come first.

4. Alerts on Unreachable Drives

Checks CGPA, active backlogs, tier rules, and mock-interview readiness. If a target job cannot be reached before the deadline, it warns you and suggests pivoting.

Strict Anti-Bluff Grading & Skill Verification Loop

Fair & Evidence-Based
No Credit for Bluffing

Simply repeating words from the question, dropping buzzwords, writing vague filler, or giving wrong definitions earns very low marks—even if your grammar sounds confident. Technical accuracy carries the primary weight and caps the total score when facts are missing.

Automatic Skill Promotion or Remediation

Scoring 80+ on a skill promotes it and marks it Verified; 70+ re-verifies flagged or basic skills; 45–69 flags it as Needs Re-verification; below 45 marks it Unverified. Any skill below 70 automatically adds remedial study items to your Upskill Plan and refreshes your job match scores.

Difficulty Adapts & Offline Fallback Notice

Strong answers (70+) raise the next question's difficulty toward Hard, while struggling answers (<40) step down to Easy. If live AI authoring or grading is unavailable offline, the screen clearly marks practice turns as limited so offline practice never falsely verifies a skill.
