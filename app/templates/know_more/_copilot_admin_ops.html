HITL Approvals Center (Two-Tier Governance)

Screen: Approvals
Faculty Mentor (Tier 1) TPO Admin (Tier 2)

Sensitive actions never happen silently behind a user's back—they wait in the HITL (Human-in-the-Loop) Approvals Center until reviewed by authorized staff. The system enforces two-person separation of duties (no one can approve their own request or bypass TPO sign-off on institutional items) and scopes Faculty Mentors strictly to students and drives matching their department.

All Pending Review Approved Rejected
Faculty & TPO

Where to find it: Top-right filter tabs in the HITL Approvals Center header.

What it does: Switches the queue view. All displays every item waiting for review plus the 15 most recent completed decisions; the other tabs show only that exact status.

Review note (optional) Endorse (Tier 1)
Tier 1 Review

Where to find it: Bottom action bar of pending student nominations and skill waivers.

What it does: Adds an optional decision note to the permanent record. Clicking Endorse (Tier 1) records faculty endorsement and advances linked waiver applications to await final TPO sign-off (hidden on bootcamps and resume re-upload requests, which go straight to TPO).

Approve & Execute (Tier 2) Reject Withdraw / Remove
Separation of Duties

Where to find it: Action row on every pending card.

What it does: Approve & Execute (Tier 2) / Approve & Publish (TPO Only) runs the requested change and records an immutable audit entry in one atomic step—if anything fails, nothing is partially saved. Faculty Mentors are blocked from giving final approval on nominations, campus drives, cohort bootcamps, resume re-upload unlocks, or anything they requested or endorsed themselves. Reject declines the request and marks linked applications/drives as rejected; Withdraw / Remove (TPO Admin only) closes out generic requests cleanly.

Edit Drive Approve & Publish (TPO Only) trace: ...
TPO Admin Only

Where to find it: Pending Campus Drive Publish cards (submitted via Smart JD or Recruiter JNF).

What it does: Displays the SOP-01 salary tier badge, Recruiter JNF or TPO Smart JD origin badge, parser engine used, CGPA/Branches/Backlogs/Batch grid, and a clickable trace badge that jumps directly to the Audit Trail filtered to that drive. Clicking Edit Drive opens the Smart JD editor so the TPO Admin can refine fields before publishing.

What Each Approval Type Automatically Unlocks Once Approved

1. Job Nomination

Adds an official nomination milestone to the student's progress timeline and advances their drive application status to Nominated.

2. Skill Proficiency Waiver

Upgrades the student's skill proficiency level and immediately recalculates their job match scores and active application readiness.

3. Student Resume Re-upload Unlock

Grants the student a one-time unlock on their Profile page to upload a new resume (automatically re-locks right after they upload).

4. Campus Drive Publish

Publishes the job into live On-Campus Opportunities and launches a background task to re-score the entire student cohort against it.

5. Cohort Bootcamp Publish

Selects the best matching course from the catalog, publishes pending bootcamp tasks across student upskilling plans, updates total plan hours, and reports how many students are impacted.

Background Tasks & Health Checks

Non-Blocking

Heavy jobs—such as fetching live job boards, scanning image resumes with OCR, and re-scoring the whole student cohort when a new Campus Drive is published—run quietly in the background (tracking queued, running, completed, or failed with live progress) so screens never freeze. By default it uses built-in local worker threads, or Redis when configured (automatically falling back to local threads if Redis is offline).

Two built-in health checks monitor uptime: a fast liveness check and a 5-point readiness check (verifying database connection, 124+ taxonomy skills, 18+ SOPs, 220+ interview questions, and the 384-dim text embedder).

Offline AI vs. Cloud AI Modes

Zero-Key Ready

Offline AI Mode (Default): Uses the built-in deterministic offline assistant and local sentence embeddings, requiring no internet connection and no paid API keys.

Cloud AI Mode: Can be configured to use AWS Bedrock (e.g., Amazon Nova Lite) or OpenAI-compatible models while tracking token usage and estimated USD cost. If a cloud AI service fails 3 times within 60 seconds, a 30-second safety circuit breaker automatically switches normal tasks to the offline assistant so users never wait on a stalled network.

Terminal Setup & Admin Commands

Operator Tools
  • Seed Database (seed): Sets up database tables, loads skills, jobs, and courses; adding the demo-cohort option seeds 12 JKLU students with matches, plans, interviews, and pending approvals.
  • Offline Resume Match (demo): Tests matching a single resume file against the built-in job catalog without a database.
  • Scrape & Import Jobs (scrape-jobs / ingest-output): Fetches live Indian tech jobs by keyword and city or imports a saved job file.
  • Clear Live Jobs (purge-live-jobs): Removes externally scraped jobs and resets temporary scraper caches.
  • Quality & Model Warmup (eval-benchmark / warm-cache): Runs the 4-part system benchmark or pre-warms the local offline embedding model.
