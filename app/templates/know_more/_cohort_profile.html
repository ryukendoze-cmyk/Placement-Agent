Technical Competencies & Verification Badges
Advanced Intermediate Basic

Skills are grouped by category. Each chip shows the skill name, how many times it appeared in the resume (such as x2), its proficiency level, a hover tooltip with the exact resume evidence, and one of these verification badges:

Verified

Proven in a mock interview (displays the interview score when available).

Claimed

Detected from the resume, not yet tested in a mock interview.

Needs Re-verification

Flagged during a mock interview for a follow-up check. Includes a Re-verify Skill link.

Unverified

Marked as a remedial gap after an interview. Includes a Re-verify Skill link.

Request TPO Permission
Student (Locked State)

Where: Resume Refresh card after a student's first resume is processed (shows Resume Locked After Initial Verification). What it does: Sends a one-time unlock request to the TPO Approvals queue and displays a Pending TPO Approval banner. Once approved, the student can upload one updated resume, after which the box automatically re-locks.

Audit Resume Bullets Resume X-Ray & AI Bullet Improver
Agent Brain Quick Action

Where: Inside the Agent Brain drawer (for students) or by asking the AI assistant to audit your resume. What it does:

  • Resume X-Ray: Calculates a 0–100 ATS readiness score across four pillars — Impact Quantification (30%), Action Verb Strength (25%), Skill Density (25%), and Section Completeness (20%) — and checks detected sections (Education, Projects, Experience, Achievements) and profile links.
  • AI Bullet Improver: Finds up to 3 weak bullet points (missing numbers or strong action verbs) and rewrites them into impact-focused STAR-method bullets using only skills and projects already in the resume, never inventing new skills or metrics.
