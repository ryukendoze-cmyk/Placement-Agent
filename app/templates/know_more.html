Architecture Overview

End-to-End JKLU CareerOS Pipeline

8-Stage Agentic Flow

Every student lifecycle event flows deterministically and auditably through eight modular stages, combining local embeddings, OR-Tools optimization, BKT mastery tracking, grounded SOP RAG, and Human-in-the-Loop governance.

1. Resume Ingestion → 2. Taxonomy Skill Extraction → 3. Vector & Hybrid Job Matching → 4. CP-SAT/BKT Upskill Planner → 5. Adaptive Mock Interview → 6. Policy RAG Copilot → 7. HITL Approvals & Campus Drives → 8. Cohort Analytics & Audit

Pipeline Stage Inventory & Core Subsystems

All Roles
Stage Subsystem & Modules Key Mechanism Primary Output
1. Resume Ingestion app/api/routes/students.py, pages.py Parses PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD, or Image resumes; validates re-upload unlock gate for students. Student row + raw resume text
2. Taxonomy Skill Extraction app/skills/extract.py, app/taxonomy/ Maps raw phrases onto canonical JKLU skill taxonomy & branch codes with confidence/evidence provenance. StudentSkill chips + student vector
3. Vector & Hybrid Job Matching app/matching/engine.py, app/embeddings/ Combines skill overlap, cosine embedding similarity, eligibility gates, and Dream/Core/Tier-2 bands. Ranked JobMatch scores & gaps
4. CP-SAT / BKT Upskill Planner app/planning/planner.py, bkt.py Schedules prerequisite-aware learning roadmaps under weekly hour budgets and updates mastery via BKT quizzes. UpskillPlan + PlanItem timeline
5. Adaptive Mock Interview app/interview/, routes/interviews.py 5-turn adaptive technical interview with STT/TTS voice mode, anti-bluff rubric, and skill verification upgrades. Interview score & verified skills
6. Policy RAG Copilot app/rag/policy_rag.py, routes/copilot.py Retrieves institutional SOP-01..SOP-18+ rules with deterministic offline answers and verified citations. Grounded answer + [SOP-XX] pills
7. HITL Approvals & Campus Drives routes/approvals.py, campus_drives.py Queues sensitive actions (bootcamps, waivers, re-uploads, JNF submissions, drives) for faculty/TPO review. ApprovalRequest + Campus Drives
8. Cohort Analytics & Audit app/analytics/, routes/admin.py Aggregates executive KPIs, readiness matrices, drive simulations, and append-only AuditLog traces. Analytics charts + immutable audit ledger
