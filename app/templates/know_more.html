Chapter 1 · Big Picture

How CareerOS Works

8-Step Student Journey

Every student’s placement journey moves through eight clear, connected steps — from uploading a resume on day one to sitting for campus drives and tracking batch-wide placement readiness.

1. Resume Upload → 2. Skill Detection → 3. Job Matching → 4. Upskill Plan → 5. Mock Interview → 6. Placement Copilot → 7. Approvals & Campus Drives → 8. Placement Analytics
STEP 1–8

The 8-Step Student Placement Journey

All Users
Step 1 · Resume Upload Home → Onboarding / Profile

A placement officer uploads a student’s resume (PDF, Word, Text, Markdown, or Image). Personal contact details like phone numbers and emails are automatically hidden before AI reads the document. Students can request a one-time unlock from the TPO whenever they need to upload an updated resume.

Step 2 · Skill Detection Student Profile → Skills

CareerOS automatically detects the student’s name, branch, year, GPA, and technical skills, matching them against JKLU’s standardized 124-skill catalog. Skills found on a resume start as Claimed until the student proves them in a quiz or interview.

Step 3 · Job Matching Matches Screen

Every new resume or newly added job triggers instant matching. Openings are ranked by skill overlap, branch and GPA eligibility, and salary tier (Dream, Core, or Tier-2), clearly highlighting which skills are already covered and which ones are missing.

Step 4 · Upskill Plan Plan & Progress Screens

To close skill gaps, CareerOS builds a realistic week-by-week study schedule tailored to the student’s available hours per week and topic prerequisites. Students complete tasks, take short verification quizzes, swap topics, or request faculty waivers.

Step 5 · Mock Interview Mock Interview Studio

Students practice with a 5-question adaptive technical interview using either microphone voice or typed answers. A strict anti-bluff rubric grades accuracy, depth, and clarity — upgrading strong performances to Verified skills on their profile.

Step 6 · Placement Copilot Placement Copilot

Anyone with questions about dream offers, GPA cutoffs, attendance rules, or company eligibility can ask the Placement Copilot. Answers stream word-by-word with clickable institutional policy citations so nothing is guessed or fabricated.

Step 7 · Approvals & Campus Drives Approvals & Public Recruiter Link

Recruiters submit campus job details using a shareable public form. Sensitive actions — including job nominations, skill waivers, resume unlock requests, campus drive launches, and cohort bootcamps — pass through Faculty endorsement and TPO approval.

Step 8 · Placement Analytics Analytics & Audit Trail

Placement officers and faculty mentors monitor batch readiness charts, test drive eligibility cutoffs with a live simulator, manage institutional placement rules, and inspect a tamper-evident activity log of every important action.

Roles

Who Can Use Each Part of CareerOS

All Users
Student Personal Workspace

Views only their own profile, skill badges, matched jobs, upskill schedule, progress charts, and mock interviews. Can submit job applications, request skill waivers or resume re-uploads, and ask Placement Copilot about their own eligibility.

Faculty Mentor Department Scope

Views students and analytics filtered to their assigned department, runs mock interviews or review plans for mentees, and performs Tier-1 Endorsements on student requests before the TPO gives final sign-off.

TPO Admin Full Placement Office

Manages the entire cohort: onboards resumes, scrapes live jobs, runs the Drive Eligibility Simulator, gives final Tier-2 Approvals, configures institutional policies, inspects the Audit Trail, and accesses this User Guide.

Recruiter Shareable JNF Link

Opens the public Job Notification Form link shared by the placement cell — no account needed — to submit company role details, CTC package, required skills, and branch/GPA criteria for TPO verification.
